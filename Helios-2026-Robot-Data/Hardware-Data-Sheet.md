## 7. Current Limits & Neutral (Brake/Coast) Modes — recommended starting config

Three tiers per motor: **Conservative START** (write these first — team preference is to begin low), **Regular** (raise toward these once a mechanism proves it needs the torque), **Cap** (never exceed — tied to motor stall / 40 A breaker / the brownout budget). Grounded in the team's brownout math (roboRIO browns out ~310 A total; Kraken X60 stalls 366 A, X44 ~275 A, NEO 550 stalls 100 A). Format `St`=stator / `Sup`=supply for Krakens; single number = `smartCurrentLimit` for NEOs. **Err low: too low = a weak mechanism (obvious, harmless); too high = brownout or burn.** Today: nothing is limited except steer (40 A stator).

| Motor (CAN) | Model | Conservative START | Regular (tune target) | Cap (never exceed) | Neutral |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drive ×4 (2,5,8,11) | Kraken X60 | St 40 / Sup 30 | St 120 / Sup 60 | St 120 / Sup 70 | **Brake** |
| Steer ×4 (1,4,7,10) | Kraken X44 | St 30 / Sup 20 | St 40 / Sup 25 | St 60 / Sup 40 | Brake |
| Flywheel ×4 (13–16) | Kraken X60 | St 40 / Sup 25 | St 80 / Sup 30 | St 120 / Sup 40 | **Coast** ⚠ |
| Kicker (17) | Kraken X60 | St 30 / Sup 20 | St 40 / Sup 20 | St 80 / Sup 40 | Coast |
| Intake front rollers (18) | **Kraken X44** (TalonFX) | St 30 / Sup 20 | St 40 / Sup 25 | St 60 / Sup 40 | Coast |
| Hood (19) | NEO 550 | 20 | 20 | **30** (hard ceiling — fragile) | **Brake** |
| Hopper A/B/C (20,21,?) | NEO 2.0 | 20 | 40 | 60 | Coast |
| Intake slider/jackshaft (22) | **NEO 2.0** (was labeled V1.1) | 20 | 30–40 | 60 | **Brake** |

Notes on the tiers: **Conservative** = bring-up defaults; everything moves, nothing browns out, raise only what's underpowered. **Cap** = the ceiling before the breaker trips / brownout / (NEO 550) burnout — don't go past it even when tuning. Drive/flywheel **supply** caps are deliberately low because they multiply ×4. The NEO 550 hood floor and ceiling are close (20–30) on purpose: a 550 has almost no thermal margin.

**Neutral-mode rule:** **Brake** = must hold position (drive, steer, hood, slider). **Coast** = should free-spin down (flywheels — braking 4 Krakens dumps regen current + stresses the gearbox; rollers). Factory default is **Coast** for both vendors, so brake mechanisms must be set explicitly.

**How it's set (one line each):**
- TalonFX: `config.MotorOutput.NeutralMode = NeutralModeValue.Brake;` (or `.Coast`) — `com.ctre.phoenix6.signals.NeutralModeValue`
- SPARK MAX: `config.idleMode(IdleMode.kBrake);` (or `.kCoast`) — `com.revrobotics.spark.config.SparkBaseConfig.IdleMode`

> Current-limit values are community-standard starting points (NEO 550 = 20 A, NEO = 40 A from REV locked-rotor guidance; Kraken supply from the brownout math) — **not** per-mechanism-verified. Followers enforce their **own** device configs, so apply limits + neutral mode to **all 4** flywheels individually. Also add ramp rates (§5.2 of the power checklist) so shooter spin-up doesn't spike. See `frc-robot-coder/power-and-safety-checklist.md` §4–5 for the full budget.

> **Biggest electrical risks today:** (1) 4 unlimited flywheel Krakens + kicker + 4 drive Krakens → simultaneous spin-up/accel can brown out the RIO; (2) the **NEO 550 hood** has *no config at all* and can pin a hardstop — a 550 dies in seconds at stall; (3) the slider can run forever (`stowSliderCommand` has no timeout) into a stall.
